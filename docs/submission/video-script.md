# Unstuck demo video: shot list

**Target: 2:59 or less.** Times are an edit plan, not measured run time. Model waits are cut, and each cut stays visible (a short dissolve). Show only real app states and real Sandbox responses. If a shot's result differs on the day, show what the app actually did and change the voiceover to match.

Persistent caption: **PayPal Sandbox | Fictional clients and documents | Simulated client replies**. In email shots add **Compose only. You send it yourself. No email is sent in this demo.** No credentials, raw logs or real customer data on screen.

## Pre-recording checklist

- **Environment.** Record against the public deployment, or a local server with `UNSTUCK_MODE=public`, `UNSTUCK_SESSION_SECRET` set (32+ characters) and the real Sandbox keys. Do not record in local mode: the shared Sandbox already has an older Signal "episode cards" invoice, so Signal would end at "Nothing unbilled found" (T14 M2).
- **AI budget.** Check `/health`: `spent_today_usd` must leave room under `UNSTUCK_DAILY_USD`. A full take uses roughly USD 0.03–0.05 of model calls.
- **Accounts.** Use a new email for every take (e.g. `mina.take1@example.com`; nothing is sent to it) and a throwaway password of 8+ characters, never a real one. Each network can create 3 accounts per day (60 in total). Failed setups also count, so plan your takes. A used account cannot be reset: Harbor will already be investigated. Start a new account instead.
- **Browser.** Chrome at 1440×900, 100% zoom, light mode. Hide bookmarks and extensions, turn off notifications. Sign this Chrome profile in to your own Gmail beforehand, so "Open in Gmail" shows a filled compose window. **Close that window without sending.**
- **Order matters.**
  - Start Harbor (shot 2) before anything that runs **Check all invoices**. Typing "Did I forget to bill anyone?" also starts that full sweep. After a sweep, Harbor's "Find out why" is already used (T14 M1).
  - Use the case chat while Harbor's question is still waiting for approval. Once you press **Approve question**, chat is closed until the reply arrives (T14 M6).
- **Prepare off camera in the same account, before shot 6:**
  - Type "Why hasn't Quill paid?" (example chip) on the home page.
  - In Quill's case: **Approve question** → **I sent it** → in **Simulated client inbox** press **Submit simulated reply**.
  - Wait until Quill shows a proposed **Allow partial payment**. Do not approve it yet; that happens on camera.
- **Prepare off camera before shot 8:** on the Cedar Lantern Studio card, press **Check new requests** and wait until it shows "Check complete".
- **Reply text.** The Simulated client inbox pre-fills Harbor's reply. For reference:
  "Hi Mina, VND-HLD-07 means the W-9 tax form we have on file for you expired on Oct 31; we require a renewed one every year. Our weekly payment run skips suppliers on hold, so your invoice was skipped twice. The invoice and link are fine. Send a new W-9 to this address and we will release the hold before the Nov 14 run. - Dana, AP"

## Where the app differs from the requested story

- **Shot 3, stop rule.** Stop-rule rejections only happen when the model tries to *finish*, which on Harbor is after the reply (shot 6). In T14 Harbor got three, all after the reply. Before the question there is normally none. So the stop rule is shown in shot 6, not shot 3. If a run shows one earlier, keep it.
- **Shot 3, evidence quote.** The question card shows the hypothesis (**Suspects**) and the question, not a quoted evidence sentence. The evidence is visible as "Read mail: Supplier portal notification" and "Read contract: Services agreement" above it.
- **Shot 4, model-dependent.** The case chat runs the model. In T8 and T14, "Yes, send it." made Unstuck withdraw the question and propose it again with a new button; sometimes it also replied in words. If the reply differs on the day, keep what happened. A deterministic alternative is the home page: type `Send the Harbor question` in **Ask Unstuck**. The fixed reply is "Chat cannot approve or send. Review and approve on the case, then send from your own email." with a Harbor link.
- **Shot 6, PayPal action.** Harbor proposes a **reply email**, not a PayPal action. Approving it only shows compose links, with no PayPal change. The PayPal re-read comes from Quill's **Allow partial payment**, prepared off camera. The USD 2,000 minimum is a setting, not a payment; USD 2,800 stays due. If Quill doesn't produce this proposal, show the real state and say: "The proposed next step still needs review. A model conclusion does not prove a payment setting changed."
- **Shot 7, "Did I forget to bill anyone?"** That chip starts **Check all invoices**: every invoice plus every unbilled check, about 170 s, more model calls. Use the Signal card button for the take. If you prefer the chip, record it last and cut the wait. Creation with per-account recipient aliases was not re-observed after T9. If the take ends without a proposal (e.g. "Nothing unbilled found", or a refusal), show that state and change the voiceover.
- **Shot 8, Cedar.** The Cedar check takes longer than this shot, so it is prepared off camera. The chip "Is Cedar's new request extra work?" also starts it.

## Wording rules (unchanged from earlier reviews)

- **Earlier drafts.** In T9, Harbor and Atlas drafts needed owner correction for false completion claims. T11's first drafts did not. Use fresh footage; never present a corrected draft as a first output. Do not stage an honesty-guard rejection; that path was only tested in scripted tests.
- **Fictional facts.** Harbor's W-9 renewal is fictional client policy, not general tax guidance. Atlas's USD 500 is a synthetic payment record. A Sandbox call is not a production transaction. An accepted reminder POST is not verified delivery.
- **Separate fixtures.** Keep Cedar (scope) and Signal (unbilled) as separate cases. Never cut Cedar's request into Signal's invoice as if agreement and delivery happened in between. A rate-card price is a proposal.
- **No experiment claims.** No success percentages or cost comparisons in narration. Any experiment slide must say: **Run 6 failed pre-registered scoring; semantic re-grading was post-hoc, one grader, ten synthetic cases.**
- **Not demonstrated.** Gmail sending, real email delivery, production payments, each user connecting their own PayPal, real customer outcomes.
- The owner records, checks captions, exports at 2:59 or less and uploads.
